How it's calculated

The ADAG study formula: average glucose (mg/dL) = 28.7 × HbA1c − 46.7, or in mmol/L = 1.59 × HbA1c − 2.59. Anaemia, recent blood loss or transfusion, pregnancy, kidney disease and some haemoglobin variants can make HbA1c misleading; your doctor reads it with those in mind.

Frequently asked questions

What is a normal HbA1c?

Below 5.7%. 5.7% to 6.4% is prediabetes and 6.5% or more is in the diabetes range.

HbA1c 7 means what blood sugar?

An HbA1c of 7% corresponds to an estimated average glucose of about 154 mg/dL (8.6 mmol/L).
