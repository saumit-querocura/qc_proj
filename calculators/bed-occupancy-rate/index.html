Turnover interval(Beds × days − inpatient days) ÷ discharges

What good looks like

Many hospitals aim for 75% to 85% occupancy: lower wastes capacity, while running above about 85% leaves little room for emergencies and is linked to bed shortages and infection risk. ALOS depends heavily on case mix, so compare it within a specialty and over time.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good bed occupancy rate?

Around 75% to 85% is a common target. Sustained occupancy above 85% leaves too little room for emergency admissions.

How do you calculate average length of stay?

Total inpatient days in a period divided by the number of discharges in the same period.
