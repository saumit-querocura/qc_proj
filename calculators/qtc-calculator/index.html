Formulas

FormulaQTc =
BazettQT ÷ √RR
FridericiaQT ÷ ∛RR
FraminghamQT + 154 × (1 − RR)
HodgesQT + 1.75 × (HR − 60)

Thresholds

Prolonged: over 450 ms in men, over 470 ms in women. 500 ms or more, or a rise of 60 ms or more from baseline, carries a high risk of torsades de pointes. RR is in seconds (60 ÷ heart rate). With a QRS of 120 ms or more, the JT interval is a better guide.

Frequently asked questions

Which QTc formula is best?

Fridericia and Framingham correct better than Bazett at heart rates outside 60 to 100; Bazett remains common but overestimates QTc at fast rates.
