Free health and hospital calculators

Quick, private calculators that run in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere. For people and families, and for the doctors and managers who run clinics and hospitals.

For you and your family

  • BMI calculator with Indian cut-offs: Free BMI calculator with both WHO and the lower Asian-Indian cut-offs (overweight from 23, obese from 25). Enter height and weight; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Is my blood pressure normal? Check a reading: Enter your blood pressure reading to see its category (ISH 2020): normal, high-normal, grade 1 or grade 2 hypertension, and when to get urgent care.
  • HbA1c to average blood sugar: Convert an HbA1c result to estimated average glucose in mg/dL and mmol/L, or the other way round, with the normal, prediabetes and diabetes ranges.
  • Pregnancy due date calculator: Calculate your estimated due date and how many weeks pregnant you are from the first day of your last period, with your cycle length.
  • Make a printable medicine schedule: Make a printable daily medicine chart for a parent or yourself: medicines, doses and times of day in a clear table. Free, in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.

For doctors and hospital managers

  • QTc calculator (four formulas): Corrected QT interval calculator for clinicians: Bazett, Fridericia, Framingham and Hodges from QT and heart rate, with prolongation thresholds.
  • Bed occupancy rate and length-of-stay calculator: Free hospital KPI calculator: bed occupancy rate, average length of stay (ALOS), bed turnover rate and turnover interval from beds, inpatient days and discharges.
  • OPD capacity and waiting time calculator: How many OPD patients can your doctors see per day, and how long will the next patient wait? A free calculator for clinics and hospitals.
