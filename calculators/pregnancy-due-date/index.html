Pregnancy due date calculator

How it's calculated

Naegele's rule: the due date is 280 days (40 weeks) after the first day of the last period, adjusted by the difference between your cycle length and 28 days. Only about 1 in 20 babies arrives on the due date; most arrive within two weeks either side.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is a due date calculator?

It is an estimate. A dating ultrasound in the first trimester is more accurate, especially with irregular cycles.
