Operation theatre management

Operation theatre management with safety built into every case

Theatres are the most expensive hours in a hospital, and the place where a missed check costs most. QueroCura keeps theatre lists with clash checks, so a surgeon, anaesthetist or theatre isn't double-booked, and puts the WHO surgical safety checklist into every case.

What it includes

  • Theatre lists by theatre and day.
  • Clash checks for theatres and staff.
  • WHO surgical safety checklist: sign in before anaesthesia, time out before incision, sign out before the patient leaves.
  • One record: the case links to the patient's admission, bills and discharge.

Why the checklist matters

The WHO surgical safety checklist was shown in a multi-country study to reduce complications and deaths after surgery. Building it into the theatre list makes it part of the workflow instead of a paper form.
