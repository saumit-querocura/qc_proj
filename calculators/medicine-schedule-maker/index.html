Tips for a medicine chart that works

  • Copy names and doses exactly from the prescription; ask your pharmacist if anything is unclear.
  • Note "before food" or "after food" where the prescription says so.
  • Keep the chart next to the medicines, and a photo of it on your phone.
  • Bring the chart and the medicines to every doctor's visit.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tell me what medicines to take?

No. Fill it in from your doctor's prescription. QueroCura never recommends medicines or doses.
