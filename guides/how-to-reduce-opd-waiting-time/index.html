How to reduce OPD waiting time: 8 practical fixes

Most OPD waiting comes from one mismatch: patients are booked for the time on the schedule, but doctors run to the time of the real day. Fixing that mismatch, and being honest with patients about it, does more than adding chairs to the waiting room.

1. Measure the real wait first

Record arrival time, time called and time seen for a week. Most clinics find the wait is concentrated in two or three peak hours and with one or two doctors, which tells you where to act.

2. Book to the real consultation length

Fixed 10-minute slots fail when a doctor really averages 14. Book each doctor to their own average consultation time, and recalculate it every few weeks.

3. Give arrive-by times, not slot times

Tell patients when to arrive based on when the doctor will really be free, and warn them on the day if the doctor is running late. Fewer people arrive early and sit for an hour.

4. Separate quick visits

Report reviews, dressing changes and prescription renewals take minutes. A separate queue or time block for them stops them waiting behind new consultations.

5. Do the paperwork before the patient reaches the doctor

Registration, vitals and history-taking at the front desk or by a nurse, and patients who send their history before the visit, save minutes in every consultation.

6. Use fair, visible tokens

A token per department and a waiting-room screen that shows the queue (by number, never by name) reduces anxiety and the stream of "how much longer?" questions at the desk.

7. Watch every doctor's status live

The desk should see at a glance who is free, who is with a patient and who is running late, and send walk-ins to the doctor with the shortest real wait.

8. Review weekly

Look at waiting times by doctor and hour every week and adjust slots and staffing. Small changes compound.

Frequently asked questions

What is a reasonable OPD waiting time?

Many hospitals aim for patients to be seen within 30 minutes of their appointment time; publish your own target and measure against it.
