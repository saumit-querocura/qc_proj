Pharmacy management

Hospital pharmacy management with safety checks built in

A hospital pharmacy has two jobs: give the right medicine to the right patient, and keep the right stock without waste. QueroCura connects the prescription to the pharmacy, checks it against the patient's allergies and current medicines, and dispenses stock by expiry date.

What it does

  • Prescriptions in the visit, printable, with allergy and duplicate-medicine checks.
  • Dispensing by expiry (first-expiry, first-out), so stock doesn't go out of date on the shelf.
  • One record: what was prescribed, dispensed and billed for each patient.
  • Pharmacy staff see what they need to dispense, not the full clinical chart.

Why checks belong in the software

Allergies and duplicate prescriptions are among the most common avoidable medication errors. When the check happens as the prescription is written, it reaches the doctor while they can still change it.

Frequently asked questions

What does pharmacy management software do?

It links prescriptions to dispensing and stock, checks for allergies and duplicates, and tracks expiry so medicines are used before they lapse.
