When it is urgent

A reading of 180/110 or higher needs medical attention the same day. Go to an emergency department now if a high reading comes with chest pain, breathlessness, a severe headache, confusion, weakness on one side, blurred vision or trouble speaking. In India, call 112.

Measuring it right

  • Sit quietly for five minutes, back supported, feet flat, arm resting at heart level.
  • No caffeine, smoking or exercise in the 30 minutes before.
  • Take two readings a minute apart and use the average.
  • Home readings run lower than clinic ones: 135/85 at home is roughly equivalent to 140/90 in a clinic.

Frequently asked questions

What is normal blood pressure?

Under the ISH 2020 guideline, below 130/85 is normal for adults. Hypertension starts at 140/90 on repeated clinic readings.

Is 140/90 high blood pressure?

Yes, 140/90 or above on repeated readings is grade 1 hypertension. Talk to your doctor; do not start or change medicines on your own.
