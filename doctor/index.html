Questions

How do you verify me?

We check your registration number with the council you choose. Until then you can sign in, set your hours and fees and add patients; connecting patients' QueroCura apps switches on once you're verified.

I work at two places. Can I use both?

Yes. Your own practice and each clinic you join are separate accounts with separate patient lists, so records never mix. Use a different e-mail address for each one.

Can my clinic see my patients?

Inside a clinic, records belong to that clinic's patients and follow its roles. In your own practice, only you and anyone you invite can see them.

Does QueroCura give medical advice?

No. It organises your practice and shows patterns with the reason for each. Clinical decisions stay yours, and patients are never told which medicine to take.
