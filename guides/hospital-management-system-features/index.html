Hospital management system features: the complete list

A hospital management system is a set of modules that follow the patient from the front desk to discharge and the money from service to collection. Here is every module a full HMS should have, and what each should do. Use it as a checklist when comparing systems.

Plan my hospital setupHospital management system
Built for India: UPI, PM-JAY, CGHSABHA readyConsent firstAudit log of every access

Front desk and out-patients

ModuleShould do
RegistrationOne record and MRN per patient, deduplicated
AppointmentsDoctors' days side by side; real wait times
OPD queueFair tokens; a private waiting-room display
ConsultationNotes, orders, prescriptions with safety checks

Emergency and in-patients

ModuleShould do
EmergencyFive-level triage, time targets, early-warning scores
Admission and bedsLive bed board; admission to the right bed
NursingVitals, tasks, shift handover
DischargeChecklist and signed discharge summary

Clinical support

ModuleShould do
Operation theatresLists, clash checks, WHO surgical safety checklist
PharmacyAllergy and duplicate checks; stock by expiry
LaboratoryOrders and results to the requesting doctor
Radiology and imagingDICOM viewing, reporting, AI-assisted drafts with sign-off

Money, management and safety

ModuleShould do
BillingBills from the visit; cash, UPI, card; schemes and insurance
ReportsFootfall, admissions, collections, occupancy; export
Roles and securityCustom roles, two-step sign-in, audit log
Patient connectionConsent-based sharing with patients' own apps; ABHA
