Hospital management system

A hospital management system that runs every hour of your hospital

It brings the patient register, appointments, OPD queue, emergency triage, beds, operation theatres, pharmacy, billing and reports into one system, and connects to the patient's own health app with their consent.

What a hospital management system should do, and what QueroCura does

A good HMS removes retyping, waiting and guesswork. Every department should work from the same patient record, the front desk should know when each doctor will really be free, and the accounts team should see what is billed and what is still owed without chasing paper.

Appointments that follow the real day

A 10:00 appointment means little if the doctor is twenty minutes behind. QueroCura watches each doctor's day as it happens: who is with them, who is waiting and how long their consultations usually take. It predicts when each patient will really be seen, highlights the slot with the shortest real wait, and tells patients when to arrive. Patients using the QueroCura app are told on the day if their doctor is running late.

Sized to your hospital, from a single clinic to many branches

  • A setup plan in about two minutes. Answer questions about beds, doctors and services; one click creates your departments, wards, beds, theatres and price list.
  • Several sites. Branches with their own departments, wards, queue, bed board and front-desk board, with staff assigned to the sites they work at.
  • Roles you design. Change what each role can do, or create your own, such as "Senior receptionist". Front desk and billing never see clinical notes.
  • Run it alongside what you have. For many clinics and nursing homes QueroCura can be the day-to-day system; larger hospitals can run it next to an existing HIS for patient-consented records.

Built for Indian hospitals

  • Payments by cash, UPI and card; PM-JAY, CGHS and insurance payers.
  • ABHA ready, with your ABDM facility ID on your organisation profile.
  • English and Hindi for patients, end to end.
  • Every organisation is verified with its registration or licence number before it can reach a patient.

Security and patient consent

Staff sign in with a password and an authenticator code, and idle sessions end on their own. Every request, view and change goes into a hash-chained audit log that nobody can edit, and patients can see their own part of it. Clinical text is encrypted at rest. A hospital sees a patient's app data only if the patient connects and chooses what to share, with an end date and an off switch.

Frequently asked questions

What is a hospital management system?

A hospital management system (HMS) is software that runs a hospital's daily work in one place: patient registration, appointments and queues, emergency and in-patient care, theatres, pharmacy, billing and reports. QueroCura is an HMS built for hospitals and clinics in India.

Which is the best hospital management system for a small hospital or clinic in India?

The right HMS is the one your team will actually use: quick to set up, priced for your size, with UPI billing, PM-JAY and CGHS support, ABHA readiness and role-based access. QueroCura creates a setup plan sized to your facility in about two minutes and can be the day-to-day system for clinics and nursing homes. Our buyer's guide lists what to check before choosing any HMS.

Does QueroCura support PM-JAY, CGHS and insurance?

Yes. Bills can be raised to PM-JAY, CGHS and insurance payers, alongside cash, UPI and card payments with receipts.

Is QueroCura ABHA and ABDM ready?

Yes. Organisations can record their ABDM facility ID, and patients can keep their ABHA on their QueroCura profile.

Can QueroCura handle more than one branch?

Yes. Sites have their own departments, wards, queue and bed board, and staff are assigned to the sites they work at.
