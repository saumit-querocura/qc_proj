Medicine reminders

A medicine reminder app that keeps the whole family in the loop

QueroCura reminds you to take each dose at the times on the prescriptions you confirmed. Tap Taken right on the notification, and the family members you allow are told straight away, so a parent's or child's medicines never depend on memory alone. It is free, and it works in English and Hindi.

How the reminders work

  • Timed to your prescriptions. Add a prescription (or let CuraVault read it from a photo) and confirm the doses; reminders follow those times.
  • Taken, from the notification. Mark a dose as taken without opening the app.
  • Family alerts. People you give permission to are notified when a dose is taken, for example "Aarav (your child) has taken his morning dose".
  • On your phone or computer. Notifications arrive through your browser, with no app store needed.
  • Your visits too. The health calendar reminds you of appointments and tests, and exports to any calendar.

Reminders, never recommendations

QueroCura reminds you to take what your doctor prescribed. It never suggests a medicine, a dose or a change: those decisions stay with your doctor.

Looking after someone else's medicines

Caring for an elderly parent? Connect your QueroCura accounts in the family garden and choose what you can see. With permission for Medicines, you can follow their doses and be told when they are taken, from anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free medicine reminder app in India?

Look for reminders timed to your actual prescriptions, an easy way to mark a dose taken, family alerts for people you care for, and Hindi support. QueroCura does all of these and is free.

Can I get reminders for my parents' medicines?

Yes. Connect your accounts in QueroCura's family garden with permission for Medicines, and you are told when their doses are taken.

Does the app tell me which medicine to take?

No. QueroCura only reminds you about medicines your doctor prescribed and you confirmed. It never recommends a medicine or dose.

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes. The whole app, including reminders, works in English and Hindi.
