Appointments & queues

Doctor appointment booking that shows when patients will really be seen

Most booking systems hand out fixed slots that drift as the day runs late. QueroCura predicts the real start time of every slot from each doctor's own recent consultations, points the front desk and the patient to the shortest real wait, and keeps the waiting room quiet.

How it works

  • Live status of every doctor: free now, with a patient, or running late, at a glance on the front-desk board.
  • The best slot, highlighted: every free slot shows when the patient will really be seen, and the soonest real start is marked.
  • Arrive-by times: the desk tells patients when to come, and the app warns them on the day if their doctor is running late.
  • Learns each doctor: predictions use each doctor's own consultations, not a fixed slot length.

OPD tokens and the waiting room

Tokens are issued fairly by department. The waiting-room screen shows token numbers and room names only, never a patient's name, and each screen has its own private link that an administrator can switch off at any time.

For patients

Book a visit with a doctor or clinic on QueroCura, see the time you are likely to be seen, add it to your calendar and get reminders. Visit prep helps you write down why you are going and what to ask.

Frequently asked questions

What is an OPD queue management system?

It issues tokens to out-patients, calls them in order and shows the queue on a waiting-room screen. QueroCura's queue issues fair tokens by department and never shows names on the screen.

Can patients see the doctor's real waiting time?

Yes. Each free slot shows when the patient will really be seen, based on how the doctor's day is actually running.
