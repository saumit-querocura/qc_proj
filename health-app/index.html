Works the way you do

Use it on your phone or computer, in English or Hindi. Import readings from Apple Health, Google Fit or a spreadsheet. Your data stays yours: nothing is shared unless you choose, and you can export or delete it at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Is QueroCura free?

Yes. Signing up and using the symptom checker, insights, calendar, reminders, family garden and CuraVault is free.

Does QueroCura give medical advice?

QueroCura gives general wellness information and helps you prepare for your doctor. It is not a diagnosis and never recommends medicines. In an emergency, call 112.
