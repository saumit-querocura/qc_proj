Clinic & practice management

Clinic management software for doctors who would rather see patients than paperwork

QueroCura gives a doctor practising alone, or a clinic with a few doctors, one place for opening hours and bookings, today's patients, prescriptions, bills and practice insights. Patients who use the QueroCura app can arrive with their history, readings and questions already shared with you.

Everything a clinic runs on

  • Hours and bookings. Set your hours; patients book real free slots, and each slot shows when they are likely to be seen.
  • Today's patients. A list of who is booked, who has arrived and who is with you.
  • OPD tokens. A fair token queue and a waiting-room screen that shows token numbers, never names.
  • Prescriptions. Printable prescriptions with allergy and duplicate checks.
  • Billing. Bills fill from the visit; collect by cash, UPI or card with receipts; set prices per doctor and per test.
  • Practice insights. Footfall, collections and how your days actually run.

Patients who arrive prepared

Patients using QueroCura can send you a one-page brief before the visit: why they are coming, what worries them most and what has changed in their readings, behind a link that needs a PIN and expires on its own. You see what they chose to share, and nothing else.

Grow from one doctor to a hospital without changing systems

The same software runs a hospital: add departments, wards and beds, emergency triage, theatres and pharmacy when you need them, and invite your team with the role that fits each person.

Frequently asked questions

What is clinic management software?

Clinic management software runs a clinic's day: appointments, the waiting queue, patient records, prescriptions and billing. QueroCura does this for single doctors and multi-doctor clinics in India.

Is there clinic software with UPI billing?

Yes. QueroCura collects by cash, UPI or card and gives receipts, with prices set per doctor and per test.

Can patients book appointments online?

Yes. Patients book real free slots, see when they are likely to be seen, and get told on the day if the doctor is running late.

Can I start as a single doctor and add a clinic later?

Yes. Start on your own; when you open or join a clinic, the same account works inside it.
