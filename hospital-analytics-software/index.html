Hospital analytics

Hospital analytics that tell you what to do this morning

Most hospital reports count what already happened. QueroCura's analytics compare, predict and explain: a short morning briefing of what needs attention, forecasts scored on your own history, how full your beds will be next week, how many doctors each hour needs, where revenue is leaking, and how care quality is moving.

Numbers you can question

Every forecast shows how far off it was on your own recent history, and says so when it is no better than "same day last week". Every line in the briefing states its basis. When there is too little history, the screen says so instead of guessing.

Who sees what

Analytics need the Reports permission, and summaries show totals, not patient details. A list that names patients, such as unbilled visits or missed follow-ups, appears only for people whose role already lets them see those patients.

Frequently asked questions

What is hospital analytics software?

Software that turns a hospital's visits, beds, bills and results into comparisons, forecasts and worklists, so managers can act before problems grow.

How accurate are the forecasts?

It depends on your hospital, so QueroCura measures it: each forecast is tested on a held-back fortnight of your own history and the error is shown beside it.
