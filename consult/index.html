Getting you connected

Would you like QueroCura to take notes?

We listen to the call and afterwards write up the important things the doctor said — in their own words — as a page you can keep. The sound is turned into words on QueroCura’s own machine, never sent anywhere else, and the recording is deleted as soon as the notes are written. Your doctor is told if you say yes, and you can stop at any moment.
