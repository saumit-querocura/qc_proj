Eye hospitals

Software for eye hospitals

Eye hospitals see many patients a day and run high-volume theatre lists, mostly for cataract. Their software must keep a busy OPD moving, schedule theatres tightly and bill packages and schemes cleanly.

What eye hospitals need from their software

  • A fast, fair OPD with many quick reviews.
  • Theatre lists that can hold many short cases a day.
  • Package billing and schemes such as PM-JAY and CGHS.
  • Pre-operative checks and follow-up visits on one record.

What QueroCura covers

  • OPD tokens, live doctor status and real wait times.
  • Theatre lists with clash checks and the WHO surgical safety checklist.
  • Pharmacy with allergy checks and stock by expiry.
  • Billing by cash, UPI, card, PM-JAY, CGHS and insurance.
  • One record per patient for visits, results and bills.

What to pair it with

QueroCura does not include ophthalmology-specific tools such as refraction records, device integration with autorefractors or optical shop inventory. Pair it with your existing ophthalmic EMR or optical system for those.

Frequently asked questions

What software do eye hospitals use?

A hospital management system for OPD, theatres, pharmacy and billing, often alongside an ophthalmology EMR for refraction and device data.
