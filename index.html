Emergency Glass Door

In an emergency, your story arrives before the paperwork.

When you cannot speak for yourself, a care team sees what they need, verified by your fingerprint or iris, in seconds.

1

Scan

A medical professional scans the patient's fingerprint or iris — no forms, no delay.

2

Match

The scan is matched against the patient's securely stored biometric reference.

3

Access

On a successful match, the care team gets emergency data straight from CuraVault.

✕ NO MATCHNo access granted. Nothing about the patient is exposed.
✓ MATCH FOUNDEmergency data displayed, straight from CuraVault, instantly.

Who decides what's shown?

We don't. Every field is entered by the user's own physician(s), beforehand, during onboarding — the same records CuraVault keeps for you over time.

Why it matters

Every second before care starts is a second working against the patient. First responders often have no history at all.

Every access on record

Each time anyone other than you opens your Vault, it is written to your access log, which you can read any time.

About QueroCura

Health information shouldn't be scattered — or silent when it matters most.

QueroCura started with a simple observation: how much time it takes for doctors to even begin treatment, and how much hassle it takes just to retrieve a patient's information before care can start at all. We built QueroCura to close that gap.

Good to know

A few things people ask.

Is QueroCura a doctor, or a diagnosis?
No. A symptom check shows patterns that fit what you described, how urgent things look, and what is safe to do at home. It is a starting point for a conversation with your doctor, not a diagnosis, and it never tells you which medicine to take.
What does it cost?
Signing up and using the symptom checker, insights, calendar and CuraVault is free.
Can I use it in Hindi?
Yes. Tap हिन्दी at the top of any page. The menus, the symptom check, its follow-up questions and its results all switch to Hindi, and you can describe your symptoms in Hindi too.
What happens if there's no biometric match?
Access is simply denied. Nothing about the patient is shown or exposed either way — a failed match looks identical to no record existing at all.
Who's allowed to enter my medical information?
Only your own physician(s), during onboarding. QueroCura never generates or guesses what goes into your record — it's exactly what your doctor put there.
How is CuraVault different from a regular medical records app?
Most records apps store a file. CuraVault stores it, encrypts it so nobody — including us — can read it directly, and analyzes it over time to surface trends, all behind the same biometric gate as Emergency Glass Door.
Can I look after my parents or children?
Yes. The family garden lets you manage profiles for the people you care for from one account, and invite someone else to help with a person's care.
Does this replace the paperwork at check-in?
For the emergency moment, yes — that's the entire point of the glass door. A scan replaces the form.
