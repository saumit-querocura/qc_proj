Diagnostic centres

Software for diagnostic centres

Diagnostic centres live between doctors and patients: orders arrive, samples and scans are taken, and results must go back to the right doctor and patient quickly, with the right bill.

What diagnostic centres need from their software

  • Orders from doctors that reach the right department.
  • Results that return to the requesting doctor and the patient's record.
  • Imaging viewing and reporting.
  • Billing per test with clear prices.

What QueroCura covers

  • Orders between departments; lab and radiology staff see only the orders sent to them.
  • Imaging: X-ray, CT and MRI series, ultrasound, ECG and more, with AI-assisted draft reads that a radiologist signs.
  • Prices per test, billing by cash, UPI and card.
  • Results shared to the patient's QueroCura app with their consent.

What to pair it with

QueroCura is not a full laboratory information system: it does not interface with lab analysers or manage sample barcodes and quality control. Pair it with a LIS for those.

Frequently asked questions

Can QueroCura read scans?

Its imaging workspace views and reports studies, and can prepare AI-assisted draft reads for chest X-ray, ECG and CT that a radiologist reviews and signs.
