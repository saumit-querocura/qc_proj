Symptom checker

A symptom checker that asks the questions a doctor would

Type or speak how you feel, in English or Hindi. QueroCura's symptom checker asks a few follow-up questions, each chosen to rule something in or out, then tells you how urgent things look, what is safe at home, what to avoid and when to see a doctor. It takes about two minutes, is free, and never tells you which medicine to take.

How a check works

  • Say it your way. No medical words needed, in English or Hindi.
  • Cura asks what matters. A clinical-interview style: the next most useful question, with the reason it asks.
  • Know what to do next. A clear urgency level, what is safe at home, and the warning signs that mean you should go now.

What it knows

The symptom engine covers 55 health conditions and more than 130 symptoms and warning signs, with seasonal patterns that matter in India, such as dengue and malaria in the monsoon. It only shows a result once it is confident enough.

What it is not

A symptom check shows patterns that fit what you described and how urgent things look. It is a starting point for a conversation with your doctor, not a diagnosis, and it never recommends a medicine. In an emergency, call 112.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a symptom checker in Hindi?

Yes. QueroCura's symptom checker works end to end in Hindi: you can describe symptoms in Hindi and get the questions and results in Hindi.

Is an online symptom checker accurate?

No symptom checker can replace a doctor. QueroCura asks follow-up questions like a clinical interview, shows a result only when confident enough, and is clear about urgency and when to see a doctor.

Does it recommend medicines?

No. QueroCura never tells you which medicine to take.
