Software vs Excel

Hospital software vs Excel and paper registers: when to switch

Many good hospitals in India still run on paper registers and spreadsheets. They work until they don't: when a report can't be found, a bill is missed or the waiting room overflows. Here is an honest comparison, and the signs that it is time to switch.

Signs it is time to switch

  • Patients wait more than half an hour and the desk can't say how long.
  • Month-end collections don't match services given.
  • Staff spend hours on counts for reports.
  • You can't tell which beds are free without walking the wards.
  • You are opening a second site.

How to switch without chaos

Start with the front desk and billing for one OPD, then add departments over a month. Our 30-day implementation plan shows the order.

Frequently asked questions

Can a small hospital run on Excel?

It can for a while, but billing leaks, slow record searches and no live view of beds and queues usually cost more than software once patient numbers grow.
