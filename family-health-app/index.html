Family health

Manage your family's health together, with everyone's permission

QueroCura's family garden connects the QueroCura accounts of the people you care for, so you can look after parents, children and partners from one place. Each person decides exactly what you can see and do: medicines, records, vitals, symptom checks, or editing their profile. Updates arrive instantly, such as when a parent takes their medicine.

Real accounts, real consent

Everyone in the family garden has their own QueroCura account, so nobody's health information is held by someone else without their knowledge. A connection is made with an invitation, each person chooses the areas they share, and either side can change or end it at any time.

Everything the family needs

  • Symptom checks in English and Hindi that say how urgent things look and when to see a doctor.
  • CuraVault for each person's reports, prescriptions and scans.
  • Medicine reminders with a Taken button and family alerts.
  • A health calendar for visits, tests and follow-ups.
  • Visit prep and a one-page doctor brief before an appointment.

Frequently asked questions

How can I manage my family's health in one app?

In QueroCura, connect your family members' accounts in the family garden and choose permissions for each area (medicines, records, vitals, symptom checks, profile). You then see what each person shares with you, in one place.

Can I track my elderly parents' health from another city?

Yes. With their permission you can follow their medicines and readings and are notified when they take a dose.

Can I add my child?

Each family member connects with their own QueroCura account, and you choose together what you can see and do.

Is the family health app free?

Yes. Signing up and using QueroCura's personal and family features is free.
