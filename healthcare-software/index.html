Healthcare software

Organised healthcare software, for the people who give care and the people who receive it

Healthcare information is usually scattered: a prescription on one hospital's server, a lab report in a chat, a scan on a CD. QueroCura organises it with two connected parts: a hospital management system for clinics and hospitals, and a free health app for people and families. When a patient chooses to connect, their hospital sees exactly what they share, and nothing is retyped.

For hospitals and clinics

  • Hospital management system: appointments, OPD tokens, triage, beds, theatres, pharmacy, billing and reports.
  • Clinic management software for single doctors and small clinics.
  • Appointment booking and OPD queues that follow the real day.
  • AI-assisted imaging for X-ray, CT and ECG, on your own server.

For people and families

  • Symptom checker in English and Hindi.
  • Medicine reminders with family alerts.
  • CuraVault personal health records that explain your reports.
  • Family health with permissions for each person.

Connected by consent

A hospital sees a patient's QueroCura data only if the patient connects and picks what to share: basics and allergies, vitals, medicines, lab results, scan reports or recent symptom checks. Each connection has an end date and an off switch, and every view is written to an audit log.

Frequently asked questions

What is QueroCura?

QueroCura is an Indian healthcare software platform: a hospital management system for clinics and hospitals, and a free health app for people and families (symptom checker, medicine reminders, health records, family health), connected with the patient's consent.

Who makes QueroCura?

QueroCura was founded by Udantika Dhar (Chief Executive Officer) and Saumit Pradhan (Chief Technology Officer).
