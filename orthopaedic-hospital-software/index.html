What orthopaedic hospitals need from their software

  • OPD that keeps images next to the consultation.
  • Theatre lists for planned and trauma cases.
  • Beds and discharge with follow-up plans.
  • Package billing for procedures.

What QueroCura covers

  • Imaging workspace for X-ray, CT and MRI with reports on the patient record.
  • Theatre lists with clash checks and the WHO checklist.
  • Live bed board, discharge checklists and summaries.
  • Billing with packages, schemes and insurance.

What to pair it with

QueroCura does not include implant inventory tracking or physiotherapy session planning. Use specialised tools for those alongside it.
