Boilerplate

QueroCura is Indian healthcare software that connects the people who give care with the people who receive it. Its free health app helps people and families check symptoms in English and Hindi, remember their medicines, keep and understand their medical reports and prepare for doctor visits. Its hospital management system runs appointments, OPD queues, emergency triage, beds, theatres, pharmacy and billing for clinics and hospitals of any size. Patients decide what their hospital can see. QueroCura was founded by Udantika Dhar and Saumit Pradhan.
